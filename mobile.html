Simple SynthPick a shape and see how many frequencies are in it. Smoother wave = fewer overtones. Wave SynthTwo oscillators, a filter, two envelopes, two LFOs and a mod matrix. Stack voices with unison. Overtone SynthStack partials on the fundamental and watch the shape they add up to. More frequencies = a more complex wave. BeatEight drums, one pattern. Play them from the pads or the home row, and the grid plays them back. HarmonyOne scale, the chords it builds, and one engine to hear them through. Move a tone and the chord tells you its own name. Patch SynthParts on a bench and cables between them. Nothing is wired when you open it — the sound is whatever you patch.
