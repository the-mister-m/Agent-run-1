## 2026-08-23 — voice-cap burst race, fixed without asking Brandon
`test-p1` (S5) found a real bug, more serious than the maxDecibels one: with `noCap`
off, a synchronous burst of `noteOn` calls (a struck chord, fast run, MIDI chord) blows
past the 32-voice cap — Wave Synth hits 40, Overtone Synth hits 39. Paced input holds at
32 correctly. Root cause: `voicePool.steal()` in `/src/core/audio.js` only *selected* a
voice to steal, it didn't remove it from the registry until that voice's own async 5ms
fade finished — so an immediate retry had nothing new to see. Both synth seats had
independently invented insufficient workarounds.

Amended CONTRACTS §11.2a myself (in my own §11 section — append-only, allowed) to
specify `steal()` must synchronously deregister its chosen voice at selection time, not
at async free(). Dispatched a fix across `/src/core/audio.js`, `wave-synth.js`,
`overtone-synth.js` against that spec, required to prove the fix with a 40-note
synchronous burst test landing at exactly 32, not just re-passing the old test suites.

Same reasoning as the maxDecibels call: this is a measured concurrency/correctness bug
against an already-stated safety contract (§8's cap, §10-A's steal rule), not a music or
taste decision, so it stayed off Brandon's desk per his "no decisions from me" line
above. Bigger in scope than maxDecibels (3 files, one contract amendment) — logged in
full for the Closer regardless.
