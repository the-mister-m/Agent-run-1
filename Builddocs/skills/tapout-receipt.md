# SKILL — Tap-out Receipt (v1)
Task: continuity procedure for any long-run agent (250K context ceiling pattern). Written by:
Fable 5 session, 2026-08-19, with Brandon.

Your receipt lets a fresh agent replace you with zero loss. A dead agent with a current
receipt costs nothing. A live agent with a stale receipt is a liability.

## RULE 1 — CONTINUOUS, NOT FINAL
Update the receipt after EVERY completed subtask. Updating it is part of "done" for the
subtask. Never wait for tap-out to write it: an agent near its ceiling writes a bad receipt.

## RECEIPT SCHEMA (fixed — do not add or rename sections)
- DELIVERABLE STATE: what exists right now, and where (paths).
- NEXT ACTION: the single next step, specific enough to execute without reading anything else.
- OPEN DECISIONS: choices not yet made, and who makes them (Brandon / lead).
- FILE LOCATIONS: every file this seat has touched or must touch.

## TAP-OUT PROCEDURE
1. Confirm the receipt reflects current state (it should already).
2. Post one group message (per skill-messenger-discipline.md) with STATE: tapping out.
3. Issue "end your run". The ADE starts your replacement with fresh context.

## RESUME PROCEDURE (fresh agent)
1. Read your seat brief, then the receipt. Nothing else unless the receipt names it.
2. Execute NEXT ACTION. Do not re-verify or redo work the receipt says is done.
